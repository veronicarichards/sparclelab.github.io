<div style="display: flex; align-items: flex-start;">
  <div style="flex: 1; text-align: justify;">
    <p style="margin-top: 0;">
    <strong>Lights Out</strong> is a study aiming to understand the relationships between the combined use of alcohol with other substances (cannabis, nicotine), sleep, and blackouts in young adults. Participants will wear a wrist-worn alcohol sensor and an Oura ring and complete daily and in-the-moment surveys about their substance use and sleep behaviors and outcomes for 14 consecutive days.
    </p>
  </div>
  
  <img src="/assets/images/logos/lightsout.png" alt="" style="width: 30%; height: auto; margin-left: 20px; flex-shrink: 0;">
</div>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
The goals of Lights out are to:
</p>

<ul style="text-align: justify;">
<li>(1) Examine temporal patterns of combined substance use and their impact on blackouts.</li>
<li>(2) Examine the relationships between sleep, combined substance use, and blackout risk.</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Study Status: Recruitment will begin in Fall 2025.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
If you are interested in participating in the Lights Out study, please email us at <a href="mailto:LightsOut@ou.edu">LightsOut@ou.edu</a>!
</p>

<p style="font-style: italic; text-align: justify;">
This study was supported by a seed grant from the Presbyterian Health Foundation.
