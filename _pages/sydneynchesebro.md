Sydney Chesebro is the Study Coordinator for the SPARCLE Lab. Originally from Huntington Beach, California, she earned her BS in Psychology with a minor in Disability Studies from the University of Oregon.

Sydney's research interests span affective science, neuroimaging, and psychedelic research. She brings enthusiasm and attention to detail to the lab's coordination efforts, ensuring smooth operations across ongoing studies.

Outside of work, Sydney enjoys attending concerts, traveling, and reality TV shows.
