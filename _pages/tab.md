  <div style="flex: 1; text-align: justify;">
    <p style="margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0;">
      <strong>Project TAB</strong> is a study seeking to identify unique risk factors for experiencing alcohol-induced blackouts and other alcohol-related consequences in college students who drink heavily. Participants completed the study over six weekends spanning one semester. During each study weekend, they
    </p>
  </div>
  
  <img src="/assets/images/logos/tab.png" alt="" style="width: 50%; height: auto; margin-left: 20px; flex-shrink: 0;">
</div>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-top: 0;">
wore alcohol biosensors on their wrist and completed daily and in-the-moment surveys about their drinking-related behaviors and outcomes.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
The goals of Project TAB are to:
</p>

<ul style="text-align: justify;">
<li>(1) Examine the associations between drinking dynamics (e.g., quantity, speed, duration), blackouts, and other alcohol-related consequences.</li>
<li>(2) Conduct a theory-driven examination of social and psychological influences, and biological risk related to thinking dynamics, blackouts, and other consequences.</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Study Status: Data collection has been completed.
</p>

<p style="font-style: italic; text-align: justify;">
This study was supported by a National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism grant (R21 AA031528).
</p>
