The **Southern Plains Alcohol Research on Consequences in Lived Environments (SPARCLE) Lab** is based at the Health Promotion Research Center at the University of Oklahoma Health Sciences on the OU-Tulsa campus. We explore factors associated with young adult drinking behaviors and related consequences in real-world settings. The overall goal of our research is to reduce alcohol-related harm in young adults. 

If you’re interested in joining our team, [please reach out!](mailto:sparclelab@ou.edu)
